Tailwise: Pet Care Tracker

Privacy Policy

Effective 13 October 2026

The short version

  • Your pet records (pets, logs, health records, reminders and photos) stay on your phone. There is no account and no Tailwise server.
  • Tailwise sends anonymous crash reports and basic usage events, never what you wrote.
  • If you subscribe, Google Play takes the payment and RevenueCat confirms your subscription.
  • No ads, no advertising ID, and we never sell data.

Who we are

Tailwise is made by M3 ("we"). For any privacy question or request, email macahmed33@gmail.com.

What stays on your phone

Everything you enter is stored only in the app's private storage on your device:

  • Pet profiles: name, species, breed, sex, birthday, microchip number, vet name and phone, photo.
  • Logs: meals, walks, medications, potty, grooming and notes, with times, amounts and photos you attach.
  • Health records: weights, vaccinations, medications and vet visits.
  • Reminders and your settings.

We cannot see any of this. Reminder notifications are scheduled on your phone and never pass through a server.

Your records leave the phone only when you choose to share them, for example with Export all data (CSV) or a PDF health report. Both open Android's share sheet, and you pick where they go.

What is sent, and why

ServiceWhat it receivesWhy
Firebase Crashlytics (Google) Crash details: what went wrong in the app's code, app version, device model and Android version, and a random installation ID. To find and fix crashes.
Google Analytics for Firebase Basic usage events, such as that a pet or log was added, a reminder was completed, data was exported, or the subscription screen was opened. Also a random app-instance ID, device model, Android version, app version, language and approximate country (from the network connection). Never names, notes, doses or any record content. The advertising ID is not collected. To understand which features help and improve the app.
RevenueCat A random app user ID, your Google Play purchase receipt (product, price, dates), and basic device and network information. To confirm your subscription and keep Premium working, including offline and on a new phone.
Google Play Payment details, handled entirely by Google under its own terms. We never see your card. To take subscription payments and show the in-app rating card.

Crash reports and usage events are only sent from the published app, not from development builds. We do not use this information to identify you, and we do not combine it with other data.

What we never do

  • Show ads or use advertising IDs.
  • Sell, rent or trade your information.
  • Give veterinary advice. Tailwise is a record-keeping tool; medication doses and due dates are only what you enter.

Permissions

  • Notifications: to show the reminders you set. You can turn them off at any time.
  • Run at start-up: to restore your reminders after the phone restarts.
  • Photos and camera: only when you choose to add a photo, through Android's own picker or camera.

How long data is kept

  • Your records stay on your phone until you delete them or uninstall Tailwise, which removes them. Export them first if you want a copy.
  • Crash reports are kept by Firebase Crashlytics for up to 90 days.
  • Usage events are kept by Google Analytics for the retention period we set, no longer than 14 months.
  • Subscription records are kept by RevenueCat and Google Play while needed to provide Premium and meet legal and tax duties.

Your choices and rights

You can export or delete your records in the app at any time. Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR in the UK and EU, or the CCPA in California), you may have rights to access, correct, delete or object to the processing of personal information about you. Because crash reports and usage events are not linked to your name or account, we usually cannot single them out, but email us and we will help with any request, including deleting subscription records held by RevenueCat.

Where these laws apply, we rely on our legitimate interest in keeping the app working and improving it (crash reports and usage events), and on providing the service you asked for (subscriptions).

Children

Tailwise is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

International transfers

Google (Firebase and Google Play) and RevenueCat may process the information above in the United States and other countries, under their own safeguards for international transfers.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update the date at the top and, for important changes, tell you in the app.
